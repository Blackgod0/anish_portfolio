1. Foot Step Power Generator Using Arduino

Created a sustainable energy solution using Arduino technology that converts kinetic energy from footsteps into electrical power. This project addresses the growing demand for renewable energy by harvesting energy from daily human activities. The system uses piezoelectric sensors to capture pressure, processes signals with an Arduino microcontroller, and stores the generated electricity for low-power applications like LED lighting and charging small devices.

Technical Highlights

  • Designed and developed a prototype energy harvesting system using piezoelectric sensors and Arduino Uno.
  • Integrated a rectifier circuit and rechargeable battery to store the generated electrical energy.
  • Programmed Arduino to regulate power flow and monitor voltage output in real time.
  • Implemented LEDs as output indicators to demonstrate the energy conversion process.
  • Tested system performance under various load conditions and footstep frequencies.

Key Achievements

  • Successfully demonstrated renewable energy generation from footsteps in real-time conditions.
  • Showcased potential applications for public places like railway stations, shopping malls, and streets.
  • Improved understanding of piezoelectric sensors, power electronics, and microcontroller-based automation.
  • Contributed towards sustainable engineering practices by creating a green energy prototype.
